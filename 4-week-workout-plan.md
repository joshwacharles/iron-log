# 4-Week Gym Plan — Core, Glute Toning, Forearm Growth, Fat Loss

**Stats:** 6'0", 75kg, Male
**Goals:** Reduce lower belly/side/butt fat · Stronger, tighter core · Tone down butt size · Build forearm size
**Gym equipment used:** Cable functional trainer (rope + straight bar attachments), leg extension/curl machine, dumbbell rack, barbells + EZ curl bar, adjustable bench, medicine balls, pull-up bar

**Real talk on the goal:** Fat loss is a whole-body process driven by a calorie deficit — you can't force it to come off the belly/butt/sides specifically first. What you CAN control: (1) build/tighten the muscle underneath so the area looks leaner as fat drops, (2) add enough cardio + volume to help the overall deficit. This plan does both.

**Weekly structure:** 5 days training, 2 rest days. Each session: 5 min warm-up (jog/jumping jacks/arm circles) + the lifts below + core finisher.

---

## PHASE 1 — Weeks 1 & 2 (Foundation + Fat-Loss Base)

Higher reps, moderate weight, shorter rest (45–60 sec), finish every session with core.

### Day 1 — Legs & Glutes
- Leg extension machine — 3 x 15
- Leg curl machine — 3 x 15
- Barbell squats (or bodyweight if form's shaky first week) — 4 x 12
- Walking lunges (dumbbells) — 3 x 12 per leg
- Dumbbell glute bridges (dumbbell across hips) — 3 x 15
- Core finisher: Plank 3 x 30 sec, Bicycle crunches 3 x 20

### Day 2 — Upper Body Push/Pull + Forearms
- Dumbbell shoulder press — 3 x 12
- Lat pulldown (functional trainer high pulley) — 3 x 12
- Dumbbell bench press or push-ups — 3 x 12
- Seated dumbbell wrist curls — 3 x 20
- Reverse wrist curls — 3 x 15
- Core finisher: Cable woodchoppers (rope attachment) 3 x 15/side

### Day 3 — Cardio + Core Focus (this is your fat-loss engine day)
- 20–25 min steady cardio (jog, jump rope, or fast incline walk if treadmill available)
- Hanging leg raises (pull-up bar) — 3 x 12
- Medicine ball Russian twists — 3 x 20
- Cable crunches (rope, kneeling at high pulley) — 3 x 15
- Side plank — 3 x 30 sec per side
- Mountain climbers — 3 x 30 sec

### Day 4 — Back & Biceps + Forearms
- Barbell rows — 3 x 12
- Lat pulldown wide grip — 3 x 12
- EZ bar curls — 3 x 12
- Hammer curls (dumbbells) — 3 x 15
- Farmer's carry (heavy dumbbells, walk the room) — 3 x 30 sec
- Core finisher: Plank 3 x 30 sec

### Day 5 — Glutes/Core + Sides
- Dumbbell Romanian deadlifts — 4 x 15
- Dumbbell glute bridges — 3 x 15
- Standing cable hip abduction (ankle strap, functional trainer) — 3 x 15 per leg
- Cable woodchoppers — 3 x 15/side (targets obliques/side fat area)
- Hanging oblique raises (pull-up bar) — 3 x 12 per side
- 15 min light cardio to close out

---

## PHASE 2 — Weeks 3 & 4 (Progression + Intensity)

Same muscle groups but: heavier weight, lower reps on strength lifts, supersets on accessory work, longer/harder cardio. This is where the forearms and core really start responding.

### Day 1 — Legs & Glutes (heavier)
- Barbell squats — 4 x 8 (heavier than phase 1)
- Leg extension — 4 x 12
- Leg curl — 4 x 12
- Bulgarian split squats (rear foot on bench, dumbbells in hand) — 3 x 10 per leg
- Dumbbell glute bridges (heavier dumbbell across hips) — 4 x 15
- Core finisher: Plank 3 x 45 sec + Bicycle crunches 3 x 25

### Day 2 — Upper Push/Pull + Forearms (superset accessories)
- Dumbbell shoulder press — 4 x 10
- Lat pulldown — 4 x 10
- Dumbbell bench press — 4 x 10
- Superset: Wrist curls x20 + Reverse wrist curls x15 — 3 rounds, no rest between
- Plate pinch hold (grip work) — 3 x 30 sec
- Core finisher: Cable woodchoppers 4 x 15/side

### Day 3 — Cardio + Core (increase intensity)
- 25–30 min cardio, add intervals (1 min fast / 1 min moderate)
- Hanging leg raises — 4 x 12
- Weighted Russian twists (hold medicine ball or plate) — 4 x 20
- Cable crunches — 4 x 15
- Side plank with reach-under — 3 x 12 per side
- Mountain climbers — 4 x 30 sec

### Day 4 — Back & Biceps + Forearms
- Barbell rows — 4 x 10
- Lat pulldown — 4 x 10
- EZ bar curls — 4 x 10
- Superset: Hammer curls x12 + Reverse curls (EZ bar) x12 — 3 rounds
- Farmer's carry — 4 x 40 sec (heavier dumbbells than phase 1)
- Core finisher: Plank 4 x 40 sec

### Day 5 — Glutes/Core + Sides
- Dumbbell Romanian deadlifts — 4 x 12 (heavier than phase 1)
- Dumbbell glute bridges — 4 x 15
- Standing cable hip abduction (ankle strap, functional trainer) — 4 x 15 per leg
- Cable woodchoppers — 4 x 15/side
- Hanging oblique raises (pull-up bar) — 4 x 12 per side
- 20 min cardio to finish

---

## Notes
- Rest 60–90 sec between sets in Phase 2 (heavier weight needs slightly more recovery than Phase 1).
- The glute bridges + Romanian deadlifts won't shrink the butt on their own — they build/tighten the muscle, which combined with the cardio + deficit is what actually changes its size over time. Don't skip the cardio days thinking the glute exercises alone will do it.
- Forearm growth responds well to consistency — don't skip the wrist curl/farmer's carry work even on days you're tired, it's low-fatigue-cost, high-payoff.
- Progressive overload: each week try to add a little weight or a rep or two on the main lifts (squat, hip thrust, rows, press) — that's what actually drives change, not just repeating the same numbers for 4 weeks.
